# APA function follow-up, 2026-10-16

## T1. Synaptic genes and multi-site architecture

GENCODE v40 features and PolyASite 2.0 GRCh38 PAS-cluster counts were computed for the 11,322 directional genes with complete original covariates. All 11,322 have a transcript-end count; 10,007 have a canonical coding-transcript 3'UTR length and 10,020 have a canonical CDS length. PAS-cluster centers were counted separately within annotated 3'UTRs, terminal exons, and their union; 10,640 genes have at least one union PAS cluster. SynGO 1.3 all/pre/post sets were tested separately for C1-specific, Shared, and C2-specific against the other directional classes. Models include unadjusted, each requested single covariate, and all covariates together, with BH across the three SynGO sets within each class and model. For Shared versus the other classes, SynGO-all OR was 1.61 unadjusted (P=2.61e-16), 1.16 after detected-site count (P=0.035, FDR=0.105), 1.42 after PAS count (P=4.78e-9), 1.26 after GENCODE end count (P=2.59e-4), and 0.83 after mean expression (P=0.0039). The full model uses 10,007 genes with canonical 3'UTR length and yields OR=0.85 (P=0.039, FDR=0.059); it does not support enrichment at FDR <0.05.

Transcript-level ComBat-corrected logCPM from the same cohort was transformed to linear CPM, summed over GENCODE transcripts with a coded 3'UTR, averaged separately across prenatal and postnatal samples, and reduced to the lower stage mean. SynGO-all genes were matched 1:1 to other genes by nearest log expression within deciles, without replacement. All 1,367 eligible SynGO-all genes were matched. The log-expression standardized mean difference after matching was 0.016; Shared membership was 644/1,367 in SynGO genes and 666/1,367 in controls (paired exact P=0.420). This addresses expression support, not site-detection or gene-span balance. Among Shared genes, 30,667 C1/C2 site-region pairs from 3,678 genes have non-overlapping coordinates. Both transcripts have annotated stop codons in 5,053 pairs from 1,020 genes; 201 of those pairs have the same stop codon. Missing stop-codon annotations are NA, not coded as different. Terminal-exon comparisons are supplied per pair.

## T2. Background definitions and continuous direction

A compares 4,008 C1-specific genes directly with 3,123 C2-specific genes using the 11,322-gene complete-covariate source. It includes all 101 unique GO/KEGG terms with source Fisher FDR <0.05 in any class plus six broad sets (107 rows). Fisher and logistic results use the same C1-vs-C2 denominator; the logistic model includes detected sites, gene span, and mean expression. Two Shared-derived GO terms have no members in either specific class and are retained with non-estimable statistics. BH was applied to the estimable rows within this comparison. MitoCarta3 (adjusted OR=0.62, FDR=1.95e-5) and HGNC proteasome (OR=0.18, FDR=0.0031) favor C2, while HGNC RPL/RPS favors C1 (OR=6.57, FDR=0.000171). SynGO-all does not distinguish C1 from C2 after adjustment (OR=1.03, FDR=0.858).

B identified 2,087 genes quantified in the complete DaPars2 matrix with no directional event; 2,006 have expression and span covariates. Their median corrected mean expression is 3.71 versus 4.87 for the 11,322 directional genes. Class-vs-reference tests cover the same 107 sets for each class, with BH within class; two class-term rows with no members are non-estimable. Logistic models include gene span and expression. Detected-site count cannot enter this comparison because it is zero by definition in every reference gene and positive in every directional gene, causing perfect separation. Thus B and A estimate different contrasts. For SynGO-all versus the reference, Shared has Fisher OR=2.20 (FDR=1.19e-16) but adjusted OR=0.70 (FDR=0.0251); the reversal prevents a simple category-specific synaptic interpretation.

C used all 11,322 directional genes, including 4,191 Shared genes, and saved each gene's event count, index `(n_c1-n_c2)/(n_c1+n_c2)`, and covariate-residualized score. Residualization used detected-site count, gene span, and mean expression; the index and event count remain separate columns. `fgseaMultilevel` tested 4,969 GO/KEGG and broad sets of size 10-3,000, including SynGO-all; 38 have BH FDR <0.05. Positive NES denotes C1 and negative NES denotes C2. HGNC RPL/RPS is C1-oriented (NES=3.86, FDR=2.03e-17); MitoCarta3 (NES=-1.75, FDR=1.85e-5) and HGNC proteasome (NES=-2.17, FDR=0.0188) are C2-oriented. SynGO-all is not significant (FDR=1). Tied ranks affected 0.02% of genes; their arbitrary order is an fgsea sensitivity limit.

## T3. Protein/mRNA trajectories

Slurm job 84228 completed on cu01 using 371 matched donors (142 prenatal, 229 postnatal) and 3,597 uniquely mapped directional genes with APA, RNA, and protein measurements: 1,931 C1, 1,614 other C2, 29 C2 OXPHOS, and 23 C2 proteasome. The matched RNA values trace to the accepted corrected `expr_after_combat.tsv` (source MD5 matched the Module 01 acceptance record); the specifically named `rna_matched_fixed.rds` and `s22d_gene_trajectories` were unavailable. For each gene, the producer averaged site PDUI, fitted fixed `ns(log10(age + 1), df = 2)` age curves, and correlated PDUI with log protein minus corrected RNA logCPM. Empirical lower-tail P values use 10,000 size-matched draws from other C2 genes and C1 genes. The table provides 10,789 gene-stage correlations and 12 group summaries; two postnatal C1 genes with constant curves were excluded. Pooled OXPHOS median correlation was -0.985 (P = 0.00010 versus other C2; P = 0.0265 versus C1), but prenatal and postnatal medians were 0.335 (P = 0.409 versus other C2) and 0.359 (P = 0.685), respectively. Proteasome medians were positive in all three analyses and did not support the proposed negative correlation. The pooled OXPHOS pattern therefore does not persist within stages.

## T4. Cell composition

The Furong cell-QTL covariate inputs contain 189 prenatal and 508 postnatal samples, each with six ILR coordinates; all 697 sample IDs intersect the local original model covariates. The full model preserves the original adaptive GAM k ladder and age test, adds the six ILRs, and uses BH within each event class. Slurm job 84213 completed 0:0 on cu01. Its table has 39,580 unique events, 697 matched samples per event, and no missing or out-of-range adjusted P or q values. After adjustment, age association at q < 0.05 with the original direction was retained for 21,982/22,336 C1 events (98.42%) and 17,019/17,244 C2 events (98.70%). Within C2, retention was 132/134 OXPHOS events (98.51%) and 86/86 proteasome events (100%). Among Shared C1/C2 pair events, retention was 11,391/11,592 C1 (98.27%) and 8,030/8,093 C2 (99.22%). These are event-level proportions, not a test of cell-type-specific isoform use. The requested 3'-aware single-cell reference was not found, so isoform cell-type bias was not tested.

## T5. MAGMA and tissue specificity

The exact `magma_traj_summary.tsv` lists 19 GWAS. MAGMA `baseline.genes.out` Z values were mapped to directional genes, yielding 8,520-9,304 genes per GWAS. For each GWAS, C1-specific, Shared, and C2-specific are explicit one-vs-other-directional contrasts. Five nested models add `log(NSNPS)`, `log(NPARAM)`, and `log(gene_length)` at baseline, then detected-site count, PolyASite PAS count, GENCODE end count, and mean expression in sequence. The resulting 285 rows have finite P and FDR, with BH across GWAS within each class and model. In the full expression model, EUR_AD Shared beta=0.0858 (P=0.00212, FDR=0.0402) is the sole FDR <0.05 contrast. EUR_SCZ Shared beta=-0.00936 (P=0.814) does not support a robust Shared signal.

Official HPA RNA tissue specificity, distribution, and elevated-tissue labels were joined to the directional and nondirectional genes; GTEx v8 median TPM covered 54 tissues, including 13 Brain tissues. HPA provided 3,383 C1, 4,020 Shared, 2,542 C2, and 1,399 nondirectional genes with covariates. For 37 elevated-tissue labels, Fisher and logistic models were run with the A C1-vs-C2 and B class-vs-nondirectional backgrounds, with BH across tissues within each class/background. A has no Shared contrast by definition. For HPA brain elevation, C1 versus C2 had adjusted OR=1.49 (95% CI 1.28-1.74, FDR=1.52e-5), but C1 versus nondirectional had adjusted OR=0.94 (FDR=0.853); Shared and C2 versus nondirectional had adjusted OR=0.52 and 0.63, respectively. GTEx tau comparisons used 3,321 C1, 4,010 Shared, 2,512 C2, and 1,364 nondirectional genes with nonzero tissue expression. C1 versus C2 median tau was 0.712 versus 0.674 (Wilcoxon P=3.00e-13), and brain-peak fractions were 24.75% versus 20.54% (Fisher OR=1.27, P=1.60e-4). The HPA brain result is background-dependent; it does not establish a general Shared-specific brain signature.
